Green-forward

Field

Two people. One surface. The logo's own story, told at poster scale — big flat planes of the brand green, wide rounded type that rhymes with the wordmark, and products held in the O's stadium shape. The most ownable of the three; nobody in office furniture looks like this.

01 Logo

The mark

Two green heads over the M, one green bar through the O: two people sharing one surface. Field takes the green out of the logo and makes it the ground the whole site stands on.

Primary — on chalk / white
On field — dots knock out
Reversed — on black

The stadium

The O as a frame. Products, photographs, buttons and tags all sit in full stadiums — nothing in between round and square.

The band

The bar through the O becomes a heavy black band that runs between sections, carrying a slow marquee of the system names.

The pair

Two dots are always two — bullets, pagination, the cursor, the "sent" state. A pair is the smallest team.

08 Rules

Rules

How it behaves once it moves, and the lines it does not cross.

Motion

Panels, not particles

  • Colour planes slide in like sliding doors — 350ms, snappy.
  • The black band's marquee drifts; it speeds up as you scroll.
  • Pill buttons are magnetic — they lean toward the cursor.
  • The two dots hop once when an enquiry is sent.
Rules

Own the green

  • Green covers about a quarter of every screen — it is the ground.
  • Text on green is always black. Never white.
  • Round means a full stadium. Nothing half-rounded.
  • Giant cropped type belongs to the homepage only.
Not this

Off-limits

  • "Eco" clichés — leaves, sprouts. Our green is the logo, not a claim.
  • Green gradients or a second green.
  • Stock people or illustrated characters.
  • Thin, quiet, luxury cues — this direction is loud on purpose.
