08 — Motion & rules

Rules

How it behaves once it moves, and the lines it does not cross.

Motion

Lights up, not slides in

  • Plates fade up from dim with a slow top-light sweep, 1.2s.
  • The spotlight on a plate follows the cursor, softly.
  • The light line glides to the active item; it never jumps.
  • Page changes dim to carbon for 200ms, like a room between scenes.
Rules

Keep the room dark

  • Products always sit on a plate — never straight on the dark.
  • Green is light: LEDs, the active line, the CTA, the PDP spec block.
  • Display type stays light-weight and sentence case.
  • Carbon, not pure black — the page is a room, not a void.
Not this

Off-limits

  • Neon "gamer" glow on everything.
  • Purple-blue gradients and glassmorphism.
  • Bold, shouty display weights.
  • Lifestyle stock or busy daylight interiors.
