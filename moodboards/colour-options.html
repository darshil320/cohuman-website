01
Restrained

Deep Tide as the dark, Sunlit as the marker

Every ink block on the site (hero corner, PDP spec block, footer) becomes Deep Tide; ink stays for type and buttons. Sunlit appears only as a small marker — a "new" tag, the ° on unconfirmed codes. Nothing else changes. About 15% colour per screen.

By the guidelines

03
Wayfinding

One colour per system

The pages stay ivory and ink, but each system owns a colour: STRETCH S Deep Tide, STRETCH V Ocean, the photographed ranges Sunlit. Its spec block, index-row marker and page accents take that colour, so you always know which system you're in. About 15% colour, but it changes page to page.
